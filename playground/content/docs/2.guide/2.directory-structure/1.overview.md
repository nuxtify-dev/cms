## Three-Zone Navigation

Folders directly under `content/docs/` populate the top category bar, while pages and nested subfolders inside a category populate the left sidebar.

### Nested Subgroups

Nesting a folder inside a category automatically creates an expandable group in `DocsSidebar`.
