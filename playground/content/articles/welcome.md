Welcome to the Nuxtify playground! This is a sample article demonstrating how the CMS integration works with Nuxt Content.

Nuxtify makes it easy to manage your content with a structured data model for articles, contributors, and topics.

## Features

- **Nuxt Content Integration:** Seamlessly pull in Markdown files.
- **Structured Metadata:** Built-in support for authors, topics, and SEO.
- **Vuetify Components:** Beautifully rendered UI components out of the box.
