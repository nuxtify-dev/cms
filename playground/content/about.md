## What is Nuxtify?
Nuxtify helps independent web developers **ship weirdly fast**. Nuxtify handles the boilerplate—from content management to SEO optimization—so you can focus on building unique features and telling your story.

## Who's behind Nuxtify?
Nuxtify is an open-source, self-funded project founded by me, [David Stack](https://github.com/davidstackio).

I do all the development, design, and marketing myself.

## What's the plan for Nuxtify?
In the age of generative media, having the ability to write "more code" is no longer an advantage (or even desirable).

**My vision for Nuxtify is to be a solid foundation for web developers to build more web experiences in a fraction of the time.**

As a web developer myself, I wanted to build something that I _loved_ using. I've tried dozens of frameworks, but none of them really "clicked".

So I built my own 🙃

**And now I'm sharing it with you.**

## How can I use Nuxtify as a web developer?
My goal is to make creating and sharing websites and apps as easy and fast as possible. All while maintaining professional-level quality.

This frees up your time to focus on the things that actually matter—your content, your community, and your unique vision.
